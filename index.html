Small games for odd moments.

Little phone games for the few spare minutes you didn't plan for: waiting on dinner, a long line, the couch. They install from your browser, work offline, and never show ads.

The games

Small Plates

A word game for one or two

Plate letters one at a time, serve words, and keep turning each one into the next course: TIN, TINE, TILE, TILED. Play alone, pass the phone across the table, or take on the Daily Table.

  • Word game
  • 1 or 2 players
  • New Daily Table every day
Play Small Plates Free · works offline

Potting Bench

Keep real houseplants alive

Collect and care for twenty real houseplant species, each behaving the way it does in a real home. Learn light, water and roots by looking after them, with a field guide that explains what just happened.

  • Plant care
  • 20 real species
  • Field guide
Play Potting Bench Free · works offline

Put a game on your home screen

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the game in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android

  1. Open the game in Chrome.
  2. Tap ⋮, then Install app.

No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone.
