Small games for odd moments.

Little phone games for the few spare minutes you didn't plan for: waiting on dinner, a long line, the couch. They work offline, need no account, and never pop up an ad in your face.

The games

Small Plates

A word game for one or two

Plate letters one at a time, serve words, and keep turning each one into the next course: TIN, TINE, TILE, TILED. Play alone, pass the phone back and forth, or take on the Daily Special.

Potting Bench

Keep real houseplants alive

Collect and care for twenty real houseplant species, each behaving the way it does in a real home. Learn light, water and roots by looking after them, a minute or two a day.
